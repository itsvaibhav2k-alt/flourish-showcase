## Core Capabilities

### 1. Flora: AI-Powered Communications

**The killer feature.** Flora is your AI writing partner that learns your organization's voice and generates personalized donor communications in seconds.

**How it works:**
- **Voice Training**: Upload 5-10 sample emails. Flora analyzes your tone, warmth, greeting style, signature phrases, and formality level
- **Context-Aware Generation**: Every email incorporates the recipient's full history—gifts, volunteer hours, events attended, last interaction
- **One-Click Drafts**: Select recipients, choose a template type, click generate. Done.

**Email Types:**
- Thank-you messages (triggered automatically on gift receipt)
- Re-engagement campaigns for lapsed donors
- Fundraising appeals
- Volunteer confirmations and reminders
- Custom communications

**Why it matters:**
> A typical fundraiser spends 8-10 hours/week on email. Flora reduces that to under 1 hour while making every message feel genuinely personal.

### 3. Giving Potential Scoring

Find your next major donor hiding in plain sight.

**Three-Dimensional Scoring:**

| Score | What It Measures | Factors |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| **Capacity** (0-100) | Ability to give | Real estate, stock holdings, job level, political donations |
| **Affinity** (0-100) | Connection to you | Lifetime giving vs. capacity, volunteer hours, email engagement, event attendance |
| **Propensity** (0-100) | Likelihood to give now | Recency, frequency, gift growth trend |

**Composite Score** combines all three, weighted for actionability.

**Use cases:**
- Identify high-capacity donors with low giving (untapped potential)
- Find engaged volunteers ready for a first gift ask
- Prioritize cultivation based on readiness

### 4. Lapse Risk Prediction

Stop churn before it happens.

**Automatic Risk Calculation:**
- Analyzes time since last gift
- Compares to historical giving frequency
- Factors in donor lifecycle stage
- Updates continuously

**Risk Levels:**
- **Low**: On track, no action needed
- **Medium**: Engagement slipping, time for a touchpoint
- **High**: At serious risk, immediate outreach recommended

**Automated Response:**
- Triggers re-engagement email drafts
- Surfaces in Weekly Priorities
- Alerts on dashboard

## What Makes Flourish Different

### 1. AI-Native, Not AI-Bolted-On
Other CRMs are adding "AI features" to 10-year-old architectures. Flourish was designed with AI at the core from day one. Every feature considers: "How can AI make this faster, smarter, or more personal?"

### 2. Opinionated About What Matters
We don't try to do everything. We do donor communications, volunteer management, and major gift cultivation exceptionally well. No bloated feature sets you'll never use.

### 3. Built on Modern Infrastructure
- **Next.js 16** with App Router and Server Actions
- **Claude AI** (Anthropic) for intelligent generation
- **Supabase** for real-time, scalable data
- **Inngest** for reliable background automation
- **TypeScript** end-to-end for reliability

### 4. Designed for Speed
- AI responses in under 3 seconds
- Dashboard loads in under 1 second
- Batch operations for everything
- Cached insights for instant access

### 5. Cost-Optimized AI
- Prompt caching reduces AI costs by 90%
- Smart model selection (Haiku for routine, Sonnet for major donors)
- Usage tracking and limits

Automation:   Inngest
Payments:     Stripe
